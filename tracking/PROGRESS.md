## Mastery standard

Suggested working standard (adjust as evidence accumulates):
- **Unseen / not assessed:** no reliable evidence yet.
- **Learning:** can explain the concept with notes or prompts.
- **Developing:** solves routine questions independently, but accuracy is inconsistent.
- **Proficient:** at least 90% on multiple fresh topic drills.
- **Mastered:** at least 95% across multiple fresh mixed and timed sets, with no recurring conceptual error.

A single score never permanently establishes mastery. Recheck topics periodically.
