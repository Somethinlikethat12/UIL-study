# UIL Science Training HQ

A structured training workspace for preparing for the University Interscholastic League (UIL) Science contest.

## Mission

Build the knowledge, accuracy, speed, and problem-solving ability to compete at the regional and state levels. The target is mastery—not merely passing practice tests.

## Training system

1. **Diagnose:** establish a baseline with mixed-topic questions.
2. **Learn:** study concepts, vocabulary, equations, units, and common traps.
3. **Drill:** practice one skill at a time, from straightforward to difficult.
4. **Mix:** solve questions that require choosing the right concept or method.
5. **Time:** practice under realistic time limits.
6. **Simulate:** take full practice tests and review every missed or guessed question.

A topic is not considered mastered after one correct answer. Mastery requires repeated, independent accuracy, including under time pressure.

## Repository map

- `curriculum/` — topic checklists and learning objectives.
- `practice/` — original drills and practice sets.
- `tests/` — full-length simulations and answer keys.
- `tracking/` — score history, error log, and mastery record.
- `reference/` — formulas, constants, definitions, and concise notes.

## How we review mistakes

For every missed, guessed, or slow question, record:

- Topic and subtopic
- What the question tested
- Why the answer was missed (knowledge gap, setup, calculation, units, reading, or time)
- Correct reasoning
- A follow-up question to verify the correction

## Ground rules

- Attempt questions before viewing solutions.
- Show work for calculations.
- Track guesses separately from confident answers.
- Revisit weak topics with spaced practice.
- Verify contest-specific rules and test formats against the current UIL materials before a full simulation.
